# Stage 8b1 independent review

Reviewed 2026-09-18 against `procedural-prop-surfaces-brief.md`, its final DG80 staging section, `surfaces-brief.md`, the implementation report, review diff, actual source, and focused tests.

**Spec compliance: FAIL — changes required.** The explicit topology and DG80 render contract are useful groundwork, but physical surface replacement, attachment, shape preservation and several motion directions do not yet satisfy the binding brief.

**Code quality: FAIL — changes required.** The shared hinge/linkage helper is small and avoids per-frame geometry construction. However, factories inconsistently convert geometry into hinge coordinates, conflate invisible transform mounts with physical attachments, and tests miss decisive geometry invariants. Reported 16 focused tests and TypeScript success are acknowledged, not rerun or treated as acceptance.

## Prioritized findings

1. **[P1] Rudder skins are rotated sideways by the hinge frame.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:200-201`, `src/render/cub-mesh.ts:105-106`. `createHingeFrame` maps local X onto the near-vertical rudder hinge. Both rudder meshes retain height along local Y, then are added directly underneath that frame. The prop's additional Y rotation does not remap its height onto local X. Consequently the neutral rudders extend laterally away from the fin rather than following its trailing edge. Independent runtime bounds: C182 rudder XYZ size = 1.482/0.434/0.476 m; Cub = 1.284/0.084/0.356 m, despite the Cub's intended 1.25 m vertical height. Construct neutral geometry in aircraft coordinates and attach preserving world transform, or explicitly map the full skin to the hinge basis. Verify both hinge endpoints on actual rudder vertices and fin adjacency.

2. **[P1] Left flaps deploy upward because mirrored hinge signs are ignored.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:296-299`. Hinges point root-to-tip on both sides, so identical positive local-X angles give opposite world motion. The loop stores no side and applies the same angle to every flap, including AN2 lower wings. Runtime C182 full-flap centroid Y changes are +0.1123 m left and -0.1123 m right; AN2 upper flaps are +0.1984/-0.1984 m. Fowler translation partly masks the same wrong rotation on MU2. Preserve side/physical angle in the flap record and verify trailing-edge down on both wings across the sampled deployment range.

3. **[P1] Spoilers/airbrakes overlay an intact wing and have asymmetric deployment.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:159`, `190-194`, `312`. `wingForward` remains a solid skin across the brake region (57–71% chord); no well or opening is cut. Both sides receive the same local box placement and negative hinge angle despite opposite hinge bases. Runtime MU2 full deployment moves right panel centroid +0.0794 m vertically but left -0.0082 m; the left panel also lies on the opposite fore/aft side of its hinge. DG80 receives one hinged slab, not the brief's double-storey upper-wing airbrake. Cut actual wells, preserve appropriate moving panel topology, transform both sides consistently, and verify both positive/negative roll-spoiler commands and symmetric airbrake extension.

4. **[P1] Cub wing loses its inboard/tip trailing skin and its aileron split does not follow the rounded tip.** `src/render/cub-mesh.ts:49-57`, `92-93`. Multiplying every chord sample by .72 truncates the entire wing, while replacement ailerons only cover |x|=2..5.18. No fixed aft skin restores |x|<2 or the remaining tip. The rounded fixed wing's aft boundary bends forward near the tip, while the moving leading edge sweeps aft from z=.20 to .37: at x=5.18 the original chord formula puts the .72 boundary near z=.078, leaving approximately .292 m fore/aft separation. Endpoint-to-own-hinge tests do not measure this separation. Split one common original wing contour by control-region boundaries and retain the uncovered trailing regions and rounded tip.

5. **[P1] MU2 Fowler mechanisms lack carriages and a true separated second element.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:173-177`, `298-299`. The implementation adds two static rail rods but no carriage or rail-constrained support. The entire full-chord primary flap remains, and a flat box secondary element is placed inside its trailing chord interval rather than replacing that interval with a separately shaped slotted element. Both elements share one rigid hinge; the rails have no relationship to the flap translation schedule. This does not meet the explicitly required double-slotted Fowler topology or connected rails/carriages. Model real separated element skins and a support/carriage path with attachment invariants through extension.

6. **[P1] Actuator endpoint tests certify invisible mounts, not visible physical attachment.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:150-155`, `165-167`; `src/render/cub-mesh.ts:82-85`. Fixed/moving mounts are bare `Object3D`s placed at arbitrary offsets with no bracket geometry connecting them to wing/control skins. On a conventional right-hand hinge the moving mount is below the hinge by offset*.25, while a fresh airfoil's leading skin lies at or above that level; the missing horn/bracket is not represented. DG80 similarly has two rods but no visible connected drive-station hardware. `linkageError` correctly checks rod-to-mount coincidence, yet passes even when the complete mount/rod assembly floats relative to the skin. Add visible connected brackets/horns and assert mount-to-bracket-to-skin continuity in addition to cylinder endpoints.

7. **[P1] Independent miniature airfoils do not preserve the original wing contour at skin cuts.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:136-147`, `159-160`, `173`; `src/render/aircraft-components.ts:12-23`. Each forward/trailing region is rebuilt as an entire new airfoil, with thickness recomputed from its shortened chord, instead of sampling slices from a common full-chord airfoil. The forward piece closes to its own trailing edge at thickness/2 while the moving piece starts at a different thickness/2 and rounded leading contour. They therefore do not share the original cut cross-section, and the resulting wing has a scalloped/double-airfoil section rather than preserved thickness/camber. AN2 lower leading skin additionally uses rootChord/tipChord at start/.83-span (`183`), whereas its flap uses chord(start)/chord(.83-span) (`184`), so even planform cut edges disagree. Generate all pieces from the original common section and exact span station sampling.

8. **[P2] Cub fixed stabilizer is asymmetric and overlaps the elevator split.** `src/render/cub-mesh.ts:97-101`. One `profiledPanel` interpolates from z=3.60/chord=.24 at x=-1.45 to z=3.69/chord=.22 at x=+1.45. Its aft edge runs 3.84→3.91 across the entire aircraft, but both elevators independently run 3.84 at center→3.91 at either tip. At center the fixed aft edge is 3.875, overlapping the elevator by .035 m; at the left tip it is 3.84 versus elevator leading 3.91, leaving a .07 m gap. Build symmetric half-stabilizers from the same cut curve as the elevators.

9. **[P2] Differential aileron travel is tied to side rather than direction.** `src/render/prop-aircraft.ts:305`. `a.side>0?.35:.18` permanently gives the right surface 20-degree travel and left 10-degree travel. Right roll has the intended up/down differential, but left roll gives 20 degrees down on the right and only 10 up on the left. This contradicts the report's approximately 20 up/10 down and DG80's bounded differential fallback intent. Select magnitude from whether each physical surface is moving up/down, then apply side only for hinge-basis conversion.

10. **[P2] Required acceptance coverage is missing despite all-catalog construction.** `tests/render/prop-surfaces.test.ts:43-55`, `70-88`, `91-98`. Only C182 checks signs; no flap direction is asserted. Whole-fleet hinge checking covers only the right roll skin at one state, fixed-skin exclusion is a node-name absence check, and DG80 tests only a userData angle plus rod count. Cub tests motion and two right-hand endpoint distances but no physical attachment across sampled travel. The mutation checks a synthetic mount that was moved without updating its rod, not detachment of a real skin/bracket. Add actual indexed cut-exclusion/adjacency, bilateral control directions, full hinge invariance for every control, real mechanism continuity and signed DG80 combined extremes; these should fail against the defects above.

## Evidence and limits

A one-off read-only Node/Vite SSR diagnostic instantiated C182, MU2, BE35, AN2 and Cub, measured world-space neutral rudder bounds and control-centroid deltas, and closed the server. Its HMR listener reported sandbox EPERM, but SSR module loads and geometry calculations completed with exit code 0; no browser or network evidence is claimed. No production/test edits, dependencies, Git operations or original asset writes were performed. Only this review report was written. No unchanged test suite was rerun.

The helper's finite input clamp and allocation-free rod update are sound for these uniformly scaled factory hierarchies. Conventional right-roll/pull directions and BE35 mixed yaw direction have supporting code/runtime evidence, but do not compensate for the findings. BE35 remains a V-tail, P180 retains a fixed foreplane, MU2 removes conventional ailerons, AN2 removes lower ailerons and adds upper droop, and DG80 explicit mixed radians avoid double roll. These topology/contract portions can be retained while correcting geometry.

The separate DG80 signed-detent, mixer and aerodynamic implementation is expressly outside this review; it is not a missing feature finding. Root's renewed browser close-ups, whole-fleet/full-suite validation, production build and hardware acceptance remain open. This subset does not complete Stage 8.
