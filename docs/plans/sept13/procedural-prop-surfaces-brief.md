Preserve airfoil contours, thickness, rounded tips and existing materials/detail; do not replace high-detail curved surfaces with flat toy boxes. Apply effective `SurfaceState` after AP/SAS/trim (already supplied by main) with correct signs, bounded missing/nonfinite input, and no per-frame geometry allocation. Right roll raises the right trailing edge; pull raises the conventional elevator; right yaw moves the rudder trailing edge right. Do not alter aerodynamic parameters just to fit art.

## Topology

- BE35 keeps its V-tail, using ruddervator mixing with fixed leading portions and moving trailing portions; no conventional fin is added.
- T-tail rigs inherit the actual fin-top placement and cant; P180 retains the forward lifting plane, which stays fixed unless evidence supports a moving control there.
- J-3 has no flaps. AS21/LS8/Discus have airbrakes rather than invented trailing-edge flaps. The original audit's glider-airbrake omission suggestion is superseded by `glider-airbrakes-brief.md`; real flight capability is now being added. DG800 flap capability must be checked independently; legacy `[0]` is not authoritative evidence that it has no flaps.
- AN2 has upper-wing ailerons and upper inboard flaps; lower wings carry full-span flaps and no ailerons. Upper ailerons droop with flap deployment. Barry Schiff's firsthand inspection/flight report (AOPA Pilot, 2001-10-05) describes these physical features: https://www.aopa.org/news-and-media/all-news/2001/october/pilot/antonov-an-2 . It reports 16 degrees of aileron droop at flap deployment. Preserve interplane struts/wires. Do not add lower ailerons. Upper leading-edge slats are automatic with angle of attack; do not falsely couple them to flap position without an appropriate input contract.
- MU2 uses full-span double-slotted Fowler flaps and roll spoilers, not conventional ailerons. A right-roll command raises the right spoiler. Do not mistake absence of a speedbrake parameter for absence of roll spoilers. Primary firsthand MU2 flight report: https://www.aopa.org/news-and-media/all-news/2011/may/01/mitsubishi-mu-2-addictive-performance . Keep flap-mounted trim tabs distinct from pilot-commanded ailerons. Preserve tip tanks and use the existing flap-angle contract pending any separately justified calibration correction.
- Use explicit capability/profile metadata for unusual topology and absent controls. Do not fit spoilers to every piston aircraft, or remove evidenced sailplane airbrakes because old physics metadata omitted them. Preserve native substitution behaviour and avoid duplicate overlays when imports load.
